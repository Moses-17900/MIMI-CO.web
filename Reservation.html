Mimi & Co

At MIMI & CO, every visit is meant to feel like stepping into a cozy little world shared between you and our feline family. To keep our café calm, safe, and stress-free for the cats, we encourage guests to reserve their spot before dropping by.

By booking ahead, you help us create the purrfect environment for everyone. It allows our team to prepare a clean, comfy lounge, give our cats enough rest between play sessions, and make sure each guest gets quality bonding time.

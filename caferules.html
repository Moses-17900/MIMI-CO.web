TERMS OF ENTRY & HOUSE RULES

By entering Mimi & Co, you agree to abide by our house rules. We view our café as the cats' home, and you are the guest. To ensure safety:

  1. Sanitization: All patrons must sanitize their hands before interacting with cats.
  2. Interaction: Do not wake sleeping cats or force interaction. Respect their boundaries.
  3. Children: Children under 12 must be supervised by an adult at all times.
  4. Staff Authority: Please follow all instructions given by our staff (Cat Guardians).

CAFE RULES

Paws on the Floor

Please do not pick up the cats or hold them against their will. Cats prefer to have all four paws on the ground. If a cat wants to sit on your lap, they will hop up! .

No Human Food

Our treats are delicious, but they aren't for kitties! Please do not share your food or drinks with the cats, as many human ingredients can be harmful to their health.

Gentle Touch Only

Please pet gently and avoid rough play. If a cat is sleeping or eating, please let them rest. We want them to feel safe and relaxed at all times.

Flash-Free Photos

You are welcome to take photos of our adorable residents! However, please turn off your flash, as the bright light can hurt their sensitive eyes and scare them.

Indoor Voices

Cats have sensitive hearing. We ask that you keep your voice to a conversational level and avoid shouting or running, to keep the environment calm for everyone.
